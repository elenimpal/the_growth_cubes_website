Accessibility statement

At The Growth Cubes we want our website to be accessible and easy to use for everyone, including people with disabilities.

Accessibility standards

Our goal is for the website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, and the related requirements of the European standard EN 301 549.

Current status

Technical improvements and automated accessibility checks have been carried out. The full evaluation of the final published version is in progress. For this reason, full conformance with level AA is not claimed at present.

The initial technical evaluation was carried out on 13 October 2026. This statement will be updated once verification of the published version is complete.

Scope of the evaluation

The evaluation covers the pages of this website, its navigation, the presentation of content and the available contact forms. It includes technical and automated checks. Additional tests with assistive technologies and real screen magnification are pending.

Known limitations

The contact forms do not send messages automatically. They create a draft in the visitor's email application, which the visitor must send themselves. Alternatively, you can contact the business directly at info@thegrowthcubes.com.

Third-party websites that links may lead to are not covered by this statement.

Contact and reporting a problem

If you have difficulty accessing any information or feature, send a message to info@thegrowthcubes.com. It helps to mention the page concerned and the problem you encountered. If you wish, you can also mention the browser or assistive technology you use.

You can also ask for information from the website to be provided to you in an alternative accessible way.
